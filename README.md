  

# **OpenPTN : Radio Survey Drone**
  
The OpenPTN PTN radio survey drone aims to create a drone platform and payload that can complete autonomous flights while sampling directional radio signal strength across the spectrum to triangulate and map radio signals . Existing radio mapping and surveying tools are inaccessible to many due to their high costs and inefficiency due to their ground-based characteristics. 
# Use Cases
Radio surveying is used for:

**Search and Rescue**
-   Emergency Locator Transmitters (ELTs) from aircraft
-   Personal Locator Beacons (PLBs)
-   Emergency Position Indicating Radio Beacons (EPIRBs)

**Interference hunting**
-   Illegal FM Transmitter
-   GNSS jamming/spoofing
-   Malfunctioning Wireless Device
-   Interference Affecting Emergency Communications

**Telemetry tags**
- Wildlife and conservation tracking 
- Model Rocketry
